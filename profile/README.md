## 🔥 Featured Project: [Iso-Vox](https://github.com/Jobix-Ai/Iso-Vox)
Our latest contribution to the open-source community is **Iso-Vox**.

* **What it is:** A Target Speaker Extraction (TSE) framework.
* **What it does:** It solves the "cocktail party problem" by isolating specific speakers from noisy environments, ensuring crystal-clear audio for AI processing.
* **Why you need it:** If you are building voice agents, standard STT isn't enough. Iso-Vox gives your agents the ears they need to hear perfectly.
